At a very young age, Mijia Chen picked up a brush and began telling everyone around her that she would become a designer when she grew up. Since then, she has drawn her way through life. She explored many different directions, including realistic portraiture, minimalist watercolor painting, fashion design, and jewelry design. It was only when she began creating characters that everything gradually came into focus and she finally found the creative world that truly belonged to her. Today, inspired by people in different professions, Mijia creates distinctive, vibrant characters, often with a humorous and lovable spirit.

Mijia Chen earned her degree in Design from Hanyang University in South Korea in 2026. Since then, she has remained dedicated to refining her drawing and design practice.

When she is not drawing, you may find Mijia traveling around the world in search of inspiration, rocking out to her favorite songs, or repeatedly taking on challenge x in Slay the Spire.

Thank you for visiting! If you would like Mijia Chen’s work to bring your project to life, please feel free to get in touch.
